About the park

Enchanted Kingdom is a theme park in the Philippines, located along RSBS Boulevard in Santa Rosa, Laguna. It covers 25 hectares and is managed and operated by Enchanted Kingdom Inc. The park is a member of the International Association of Amusement Parks and Attractions.

Why visit?

You should visit Enchanted Kingdom, the premier world-class theme park in Santa Rosa, Laguna, for its thrilling rides, immersive themed zones, and family-friendly entertainment just a short drive from Metro Manila.

Top attractions and rides

Space Shuttle

A classic corkscrew roller coaster that flips you upside down.

Agila the EKsperience

Soar across the country in a 4D flying theater showcasing iconic Philippine landscapes.

EKstreme Tower Ride

Drop 120 feet in just a few seconds for an extreme adrenaline rush.

Rio Grande Rapids & Jungle Log Jam

Cool off and splash around on the park's popular water attractions.

Grand Carousel

A relaxed ride on dozens of hand-carved Victorian horses, lovely by day or evening.

Themed zones and entertainment

  • Nine immersive zones. Walk through areas like the 1930s Coney Island-inspired Midway Boardwalk, Boulderville for kids, and the Victorian-era Victoria Park.
  • Live shows and fireworks. Watch musical performances, street dances, and spectacular nighttime fireworks.
  • Character meet-and-greets. Meet the park's resident wizard mascot, Eldar the Wizard.

Travel tips

Planning and tickets

  • Buy online early: get day passes on the official website or Klook to skip ticket-booth lines.
  • Choose weekdays: Tuesday to Friday has shorter ride waits than weekends.
  • Consider an EKspress Pass: a fast-track pass helps you bypass long weekend queues.

Commute guide

  1. Board the bus: go to the JAC Liner or BBL Transit terminals near LRT Gil Puyat Station and take a bus bound for Balibago Complex (about ₱102, 50 to 90 minutes).
  2. Alight at Walter Mart: ask the driver or conductor to drop you at Walter Mart Sta. Rosa.
  3. Take a tricycle: ride to the main entrance (about ₱20 shared, ₱50 special trip).

Other ways to get there

  • Driving: via SLEX, around 31 to 60 minutes from Metro Manila.
  • Shuttle: EK occasionally runs Magical Shuttle services from points like Ayala Park Square Carpark, Makati.

What to bring

  • Power bank, cash and a water bottle.
  • An extra set of clothes or a raincoat for Rio Grande Rapids and Jungle Log Jam.
